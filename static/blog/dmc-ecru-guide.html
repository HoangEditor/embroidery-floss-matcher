DMC Ecru Thread: The Essential Natural Cream for Vintage Embroidery

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

DMC Ecru isn't white, isn't beige — it's the color of unbleached cotton. Essential for vintage and primitive-style embroidery.

Ecru Quick Facts

  • Hex: #F0EAD6
  • Anchor: 387
  • Cosmo: 288
  • J&P Coats: 5387

Why Ecru Matters

Ecru replicates the look of 19th-century embroidery stitched on unbleached linen. Modern white thread on aged-looking fabric looks wrong — Ecru bridges the gap. It's the go-to for reproduction samplers, primitive folk art, and any piece where bright white would look too modern.

Alternatives

DMC 712 (cream), 739 (ultra light tan), and 822 (light beige) are nearby alternatives if Ecru isn't available. Anchor 387 is the closest brand match.
