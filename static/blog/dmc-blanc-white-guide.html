White isn't just white in DMC's world. There are three different whites: B5200 (bright white), Blanc (natural white), and 3865 (winter white). Here's when to use each.

When to Use Each White

B5200 is the default for modern cross-stitch — it's optically bright, almost blue-white. Use it on white Aida for crisp contrast. Blanc is warmer, slightly creamy — perfect for reproduction samplers and vintage-look pieces. 3865 is the softest white — ideal for blending with warm colors.

Anchor Whites

Anchor 1 = white (closest to B5200). Anchor 2 = natural white. Anchor 926 = ecru. Use our floss matcher to convert any DMC white to other brands.
