Purple Embroidery Thread Guide: DMC 208-211, 550-554, 3834-3837 Explained

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

Purples are magical in embroidery — florals, fantasy, royalty, twilight. DMC has 40+ purple shades.

Lavender/Pale Purples (208-211)

209 (lightest lavender), 208 (medium lavender), 210 (medium-dark), 211 (dark lavender). Classic floral purples — wisteria, lilac, lavender fields.

Violet/Medium Purples (550-554, 3834-3837)

554 (light violet), 553 (medium violet), 552 (dark violet), 550 (darkest violet). The main purple workhorse. 3834-3837 are slightly grayer versions.

Deep Purples (333, 550, 3740)

550 (darkest violet), 333 (deep purple), 3740 (dark antique violet). For shadows, grapes, dark florals.

Purple-Reds (600s, 3800s)

3685 (dark mauve), 3687 (mauve), 3688 (medium mauve), 3803 (dusky rose). More red-leaning — great for roses and romantic themes.

Match any DMC purple to Anchor or Cosmo.
