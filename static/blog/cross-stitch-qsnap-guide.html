What is a Q-Snap?

A Q-Snap is a PVC pipe frame with plastic clamps that snap over the edges, holding fabric taut. Unlike hoops, they're square/rectangular, easier on the hands, and leave fewer marks. Brands: Q-Snap (original), Edmunds, generic Amazon versions.

Best Size

8x8 or 11x11 is the sweet spot — large enough for most projects, small enough to hold comfortably. Get an 11x17 for large pieces.

Pro Tip: Padding the Clamps

Q-Snap clamps can slip on thin fabric. Add a strip of felt or paper towel between the clamp and fabric for better grip. Also protects delicate stitches.
