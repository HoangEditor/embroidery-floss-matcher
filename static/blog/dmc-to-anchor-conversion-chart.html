DMC vs Anchor: the basics

DMC is the global standard for embroidery floss — 456 colors, available everywhere. Anchor is the second-most common brand, especially in Europe and the UK. They use different numbering systems: DMC uses 1-4 digit codes, Anchor uses 1-3 digit codes. The thread quality is comparable, but the color matching isn't perfect — some colors are near-identical, others are just close.
