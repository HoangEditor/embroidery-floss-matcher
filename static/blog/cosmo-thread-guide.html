Cosmo Embroidery Thread: Complete Guide to Lecien's Japanese Floss

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

Cosmo (manufactured by Lecien in Japan) is the premium alternative to DMC. Here's why it has a cult following.

What Makes Cosmo Special?

  • Tighter twist: Cosmo strands are twisted more tightly than DMC, resulting in less fraying and a smoother stitching experience
  • Higher sheen: The tighter twist reflects more light — Cosmo colors look richer and more vibrant
  • Color numbering: Cosmo uses 3-4 digit codes (e.g., 600 = black, 354 = bright red). Different system from DMC.
  • Price: $1.50-2.50 per skein (US), about 2-3x DMC's price. Worth it for heirloom pieces.

Where to Buy Cosmo

Cosmo is harder to find than DMC. US sources: Stitched Modern, Snuggly Monkey, 123Stitch.com. Japan: Available at most craft stores (Yuzawaya, Tokyu Hands). Australia: JK's Cross Stitch Supplies.
