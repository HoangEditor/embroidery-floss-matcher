Embroidery Floss: UK vs US Thread Brands, Sizes & Availability

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

Thread availability is wildly different on each side of the Atlantic. Here's how UK and US embroiderers navigate the thread market.

📎 Embroidery Floss Brands Uk

UK: Anchor Country

Anchor dominates UK retail. Hobbycraft stocks full Anchor range. DMC is available but less prominent. UK patterns predominantly list Anchor codes. Exchange rate means DMC is an import premium.

US: DMC Country

DMC is everywhere — JoAnn, Michaels, Hobby Lobby, Walmart. Anchor is specialty-only. US patterns use DMC codes 95% of the time. Anchor is an import premium in the US.
