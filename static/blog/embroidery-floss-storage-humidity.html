Protect Embroidery Floss from Humidity: Storage Tips for Tropical Climates

Home › Blog › This Article
Published June 21, 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

Living in a humid climate (Florida, Southeast Asia, Queensland)? Your thread is at risk. Humidity causes mold, mildew, and thread weakening. Here's how to protect your stash.

Warning Signs of Humidity Damage

  • Musty smell: First sign of mold. Act immediately.
  • Stiff or brittle thread: Lost moisture balance, weakening fibers.
  • Color bleeding on paper labels: Dampness activated the dye.
  • Visible mold spots: Discard the skein and check everything nearby.

Humidity Control Methods

1. Silica Gel Packets

The #1 solution. Toss 2-3 silica gel packets in every thread box. Replace every 3-6 months. Buy in bulk ($10 for 100 packets on Amazon).

2. Airtight Containers

Plastic bins with rubber gasket seals (Iris, Sterilite). Thread in ziploc bags inside the bin = double protection.

3. Dehumidifier

In your craft room, keep humidity at 40-50%. Below 40% = thread gets brittle. Above 60% = mold risk. A $50 room dehumidifier solves this.

4. No Basements or Attics

Basements flood, attics bake. Store thread in climate-controlled living spaces only. Under the bed is better than the basement.
