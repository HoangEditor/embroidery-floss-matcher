DMC 666 Red: Complete Guide to Embroidery's Brightest Christmas Red

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

DMC 666 is the iconic bright Christmas red — used in nearly every holiday cross-stitch pattern. Here's everything you need to know about this essential color.

DMC 666 Quick Facts

  • Code: 666
  • Name: Christmas Red / Bright Red
  • Hex: #E31D2A
  • Anchor: 46
  • Cosmo: 354
  • J&P Coats: 3046

Why 666 is the Red Standard

DMC 666 is a true, neutral red — neither orange-red nor blue-red. It's the red you picture when someone says "red." It's bright enough to pop on white Aida but not so bright it looks neon. For Santa suits, poinsettias, candy canes, and Valentine's hearts, 666 is almost always the pattern's choice.

Best Alternatives

If you can't get DMC 666, Anchor 46 is the closest match. Cosmo 354 is slightly more crimson. For a darker Christmas red, try DMC 321 (Poppy Red). For a lighter bright red, DMC 606.

Color Family: The Reds

666 sits in DMC's red family: 304 (dark) → 666 (bright) → 321 (poppy) → 349 (coral). If a pattern calls for 666 but you want a slightly darker or lighter red, move within this family.
