Is DMC Embroidery Floss Eco-Friendly? Sustainability & Ethical Production

Home › Blog › This Article
Published May 17, 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

Is your embroidery hobby environmentally responsible? Here's what we know about DMC's production practices.

📎 Embroidery Floss Uk Vs Us

DMC's Sustainability Claims

DMC states their cotton is long-staple Egyptian cotton, grown without child labor. Their dyeing facility in Mulhouse, France, uses water treatment systems. DMC floss is OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified — meaning it's tested for harmful substances and safe for human contact.

📎 Embroidery Thread Kitchen Dyeing

Environmental Concerns

  • Cotton farming: Cotton is water-intensive. Egyptian cotton relies on Nile irrigation — water usage is significant.
  • Dye chemicals: Textile dyeing is one of the world's most polluting industries. DMC's certification helps, but the process still uses chemicals.
  • Packaging: DMC skeins use paper bands (recyclable) but the skein itself often comes in plastic wrapping.
  • Shipping: Global distribution means carbon footprint from transportation.

Eco-Friendly Alternatives

  • Organic cotton floss: Some indie brands offer GOTS-certified organic cotton
  • Secondhand thread: eBay, destash groups, thrift stores — use what already exists
  • Buy only what you need: Use our floss matcher to substitute colors you own before buying new
