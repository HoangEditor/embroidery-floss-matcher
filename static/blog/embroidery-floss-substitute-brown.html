Brown Embroidery Thread Guide: DMC Browns, Tans & Earth Tones Explained

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

Browns are the unsung heroes of embroidery — skin tones, wood, earth, hair, animals. DMC has 40+ brown shades.

📎 Embroidery Floss Skin Tones

Light Tans & Beiges (300-400, 738-842)

738 (lightest tan), 739 (ultra light tan), 437 (light tan), 436 (tan). Skin base tones, sand, light wood.

Medium Browns (400-433, 840-841)

434 (medium brown), 433 (medium dark), 801 (dark coffee), 840 (medium beige brown). Tree bark, leather, hair.

Dark Browns (300-3371)

3371 (black-brown), 3031 (very dark brown), 938 (dark coffee). Shadows, dark wood, deep earth.

Specialty Browns

  • Red-browns (300-301, 400): Mahogany, cherry wood
  • Gold-browns (676-680, 729): Wheat, straw, old gold
  • Gray-browns (3021-3024): Driftwood, stone

Match any DMC brown to Anchor, Cosmo, J&P Coats.
