Hand-Dyed Embroidery Floss Guide: The Best Indie Thread Brands

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

Hand-dyed floss transforms your stitching with subtle color variations that mass-produced thread can't match. Here's the indie thread guide.

Top Hand-Dyed Floss Brands

Weeks Dye Works

Overdyed 6-strand cotton. Slightly thinner than DMC, soft matte finish. Colors are named (not numbered): "Moss," "Crimson," "Chestnut." Beautiful variegation — each skein is unique. ~180 colors.

Gentle Art (Sampler Threads)

Historically-inspired colors — muted, dusty, antique-feeling. Named colors like "Apple Cider" and "Brick Path." 6-strand cotton, slightly thicker than Weeks. ~200 colors.

Classic Colorworks

Previously "Crescent Colours." Hand-dyed with rich, saturated tones. Slightly thicker than DMC. ~200 colors with creative names ("Belle Soie" silk line is premium).

Hand-Dyed vs DMC: When to Use Which

Use DMC when following a pattern that specifies DMC codes. Use hand-dyed when you want a unique, artisanal look — especially for samplers, primitive designs, and display pieces where the variegation adds character.

Converting DMC to Hand-Dyed

There's no direct conversion — hand-dyed colors don't have DMC equivalents. Use our 456-color DMC grid to visually compare with hand-dyed skeins and find the closest match.
