DMC 995 vs 939: Which Dark Blue is Right for Your Project?

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

DMC 995 and 939 are both dark blues — but they're different. Here's how to choose.

995 — Dark Electric Blue

  • Undertone: Slightly violet/purple
  • Vibe: Vibrant, modern, energetic
  • Best for: Night skies, electric themes, bold contrast
  • Family: 995 (dark) → 996 (medium) → 3843 (light)

939 — Dark Navy Blue

  • Undertone: Neutral to slightly green
  • Vibe: Classic, sophisticated, deep
  • Best for: Ocean depths, formal designs, shadows
  • Family: 939 (dark) → 931 (medium) → 932 (light)

Side-by-Side

Hold them next to each other: 995 is brighter, slightly more purple. 939 is deeper, more neutral-dark. In a pattern, 995 pops more. 939 recedes into the background (good for shading).

Substitution Guide

If your pattern calls for 995 and you only have 939 — they're close enough for small areas (under 1 inch). For large blocks of color, buy the right one. Use our floss matcher to find both colors' Anchor and Cosmo equivalents.
