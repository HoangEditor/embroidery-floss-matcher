Best Embroidery Thread Brands in the UK: Where to Buy & Conversion Guide

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

Anchor dominates the UK market, but DMC is widely available too. Here's the UK sourcing guide.

📎 Embroidery Floss Uk Vs Us

Anchor in the UK

  • Hobbycraft: Nationwide chain, good Anchor selection. ~£0.90/skein.
  • John Lewis: Limited Anchor range, but reliable.
  • Online: LoveCrafts, Wool Warehouse, SewandSo — all stock full Anchor range.

DMC in the UK

Slightly less common than Anchor but available at Hobbycraft, larger John Lewis stores, and online at LoveCrafts, Lakeside Needlecraft, and Enchanted Needle.

Cosmo & Specialty Threads

Harder to find. Try: The Nimble Thimble, Sparklies, and online at Etsy UK. Cosmo is imported from Japan — expect 50-100% markup vs DMC.
