Embroidery Needle Minders: What They Are & Why Every Stitcher Needs One

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

If you've ever lost a needle in the couch, you need a needle minder. Here's everything about this essential embroidery accessory.

What is a Needle Minder?

A needle minder is a magnetic accessory — two strong magnets with a decorative top piece. The top magnet sits on your fabric, the bottom magnet underneath. Your needle sticks to the top magnet when not in use. Never lose a needle again.

Why You Need One

  • Safety: No lost needles in furniture, carpet, or (horror) your lap
  • Convenience: Park your needle right where you're stitching — no reaching for a pincushion
  • Organization: Some stitchers use multiple needle minders for different thread colors
  • Fun: They come in enamel pin designs — cats, flowers, quotes, fandom references. A small joy.

Where to Buy

Etsy is the best source — thousands of indie makers sell unique designs ($5-15 each). Also available at cross stitch specialty shops and craft fairs.

DIY Needle Minder

Glue two strong neodymium magnets to any flat decorative item (enamel pin, button, small figurine). One magnet top, one bottom. Instant needle minder.
