J&P Coats Thread Conversion — Match to DMC Colors

J&P Coats is one of the oldest thread brands — dating back to 1755. If you have inherited a stash of J&P Coats thread or bought vintage kits, you'll need to match those colors to modern DMC codes. This converter does it.

J&P Coats to DMC: coverage

We have 107 conversions between J&P Coats and DMC. Older J&P Coats used two numbering systems — old and new — and the tool handles both. Type a DMC code to see its J&P Coats equivalent, or browse the full table.

Why 107 and not more?

J&P Coats has a smaller color range than DMC. Many DMC colors don't have a J&P Coats equivalent. The 107 conversions we list are from the official cross-referenced charts.
