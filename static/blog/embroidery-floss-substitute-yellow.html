Yellow Embroidery Thread Guide: DMC 700-700 Series Yellows & Golds

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

Yellows range from pale cream to deep metallic gold. Here's the complete DMC yellow palette.

📎 Dmc Metallic Thread Guide📎 Dmc Specialty Threads Complete

Pale Yellows

3078 (lightest golden yellow), 3823 (ultra pale yellow), 745 (light pale yellow). Butter, daffodil highlights, baby themes.

True Yellows

972 (deep yellow), 973 (bright yellow), 307 (lemon yellow), 444 (dark yellow). Sun, lemons, school buses, bright florals.

Golds

3820 (dark straw), 3852 (dark straw/gold), 676 (light old gold), 729 (medium old gold), 680 (dark old gold), 3828 (hazelnut gold). Crowns, treasure, autumn leaves.

Orange-Yellows

741 (medium tangerine), 742 (light tangerine), 970 (light pumpkin), 971 (dark pumpkin). Marigolds, sunsets, Halloween.

Match any DMC yellow to Anchor or Cosmo.
