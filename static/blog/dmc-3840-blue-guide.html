DMC 3840 Light Blue: The Complete Guide to This Popular Pastel

Home › Blog › This Article
Published April 28, 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

DMC 3840 is a soft light lavender-blue — one of DMC's most popular pastels. It appears in countless patterns for skies, baby themes, florals, and water scenes.

3840 Quick Facts

  • Hex: #6D95CA
  • Family: Blues 3840-3848
  • Anchor: 120
  • Cosmo: 345

The 3840 Family

DMC's blue family runs from 3840 (lightest) through 3841 (pale blue), 3842 (medium), 3843 (electric), 3844 (bright), 3845 (dark teal-blue), to 3846 (medium electric). For gradient work, use the full sequence.
