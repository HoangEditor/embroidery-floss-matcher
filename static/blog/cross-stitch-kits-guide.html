Best Cross Stitch Kits 2025: Beginner to Advanced (Every Brand)

Home › Blog › This Article
Published April 16, 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

Cross stitch kits include everything: fabric, thread, needle, pattern. Here are the best kits and brands in 2025.

📎 Embroidery Floss For Beginners📎 Cross Stitch Vs Embroidery

Best Beginner Kits

  • Dimensions "Learn a Craft": $8-12. 5x7 designs, 14-count Aida, pre-sorted thread. Excellent instructions.
  • Bucilla "Stitch 'N Zip": $10-15. Small designs on pre-finished items (ornaments, pillows). Instant gratification.

Best Mid-Range Kits

  • Riolis: Russian brand, wool/acrylic thread (not cotton). Unique texture, beautiful designs. $15-40.
  • Bothy Threads: UK brand, gorgeous designs, excellent materials. $20-50.
  • Dimensions Gold Collection: Premium kits with metallic threads, beading, half-stitches. $25-60.

Best Advanced/HAED-Style Kits

  • Heaven and Earth Designs (HAED): Massive, detailed patterns. 50-200+ colors. $25-80 for pattern only (supply your own materials).
  • Golden Kite: Full-coverage, photo-realistic. Similar to HAED.

Kit Thread Colors
