Getting Started

Before you begin, gather quality materials. Good floss, proper fabric, and sharp needles make all the difference. DMC 6-strand cotton is the standard for most projects.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Select your color palette using our floss matcher
  2. Prepare your fabric with appropriate stabilizer
  3. Thread your needle and start stitching
  4. Maintain even tension throughout
  5. Secure threads neatly on the back
  6. Press finished work from the back side

Pro Tips

Always test stitch on scrap fabric. Colors look different when stitched vs in the skein. Use our 456-color DMC grid for digital color reference.
