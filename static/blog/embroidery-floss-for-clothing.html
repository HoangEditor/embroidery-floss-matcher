Embroidery Floss for Clothing: What Thread to Use on Wearable Items

Home › Blog › This Article
Published June 07, 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

You want to embroider a jean jacket or a t-shirt. Can you use regular DMC floss? Yes — with the right technique.

Thread Choice for Clothing

  • DMC 6-strand cotton: ✅ Yes — it's colorfast and durable. Wash in cold, air dry.
  • DMC Satin (rayon): ⚠️ Be careful — rayon weakens when wet. Hand-wash only, no heat.
  • Cosmo: ✅ Excellent — tighter twist means less fraying from friction.
  • Anchor: ✅ Yes — similar to DMC.
  • Perle cotton: ✅ Great choice — single-strand, very durable. Popular for denim embroidery.
  • Wool: ❌ Not for clothing — shrinks, felts, can be itchy.

Securing Stitches on Clothing

Clothing gets washed and worn. Knot your starts and ends (unlike cross stitch where knots are banned). Use small, secure knots on the back. For extra security, apply a lightweight fusible interfacing over the back of the embroidery.

Color Selection for Clothing

Thread color looks different on fabric than in the skein. Our floss matcher shows hex codes — but always test-stitch on a scrap of the actual garment fabric. The fabric color affects how the thread reads.
