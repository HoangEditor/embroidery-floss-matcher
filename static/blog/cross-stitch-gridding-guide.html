How to Grid Fabric for Cross Stitch: 5 Methods for Perfect Counting

Published 2025 — Embroidery Floss Matcher Team

Gridding is the #1 way to avoid counting mistakes in cross stitch. Here are all the methods.

Method 1: Water-Soluble Pen (Easiest)

Draw a 10x10 grid directly on the fabric. Mark lines every 10 stitches. Test the pen on a corner first — some pens leave residue. Rinse with cool water after stitching.

Method 2: Monofilament (Fishing Line)

Use 4-6lb clear fishing line or Sulky Sliver. Thread through the fabric every 10 stitches, both directions. Pull out after stitching — doesn't snag thread.

Method 3: Pre-Gridded Fabric

Buy fabric with the grid already printed. Zweigart makes Easy-Count Guideline fabric — the grid washes out. Easiest but most expensive option ($20-30/yard).

Method 4: Thread Gridding

Sew thin thread (or sulky) through every 10th hole. Red is easy to see. Pull out after. Time-consuming to set up but most reliable — thread won't wash out prematurely like pen.

Method 5: Heat-Erase Pen

Frixion pens disappear with heat (iron). But: marks can reappear in cold. Not recommended for heirloom pieces.
