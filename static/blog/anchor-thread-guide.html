Anchor vs DMC: Key Differences

  • Thickness: Anchor is slightly thinner than DMC. On 14-count Aida, DMC gives fuller coverage. On higher counts (18+), the difference disappears.
  • Sheen: Anchor has a silkier, higher-sheen finish — preferred for surface embroidery.
  • Numbering: Anchor uses different codes. Anchor 403 = DMC 310 (both black). Confusing if you only know DMC codes.
  • Color range: ~400 colors vs DMC's 456. Slightly smaller palette.

Is Anchor Cheaper?

In Europe: Anchor is cheaper. In the US: DMC is cheaper (Anchor has import markup). Buy whichever is cheaper in your region — the quality difference is small.
