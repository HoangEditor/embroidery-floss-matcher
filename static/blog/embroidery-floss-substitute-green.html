DMC has the most greens of any color — 80+ shades. Here's how to navigate them.

Green Families at a Glance

Yellow-Greens (700-704)

Bright, spring-like. 704 (chartreuse) → 700 (dark forest). Great for grass, leaves, spring themes.

True Greens (900-909)

From 909 (darkest emerald) → 900 (dark olive). The classic green family for realistic foliage.

Blue-Greens (500-504)

Teal-leaning. 500 (darkest blue-green) → 504 (lightest — discontinued, use 3818). Good for oceans, peacock feathers.

Olive/Brown-Greens (730-735)

Muted, earthy greens. 730 (dark olive) → 735 (light olive). Perfect for military, camouflage, autumn leaves.

Sage/Mint (500s, 3345-3348)

3348 (lightest yellow-green) → 3345 (darkest hunter). Popular modern palette — sage, mint, eucalyptus.
