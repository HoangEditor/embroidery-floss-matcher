DMC Sales Cycle (US)

  • JoAnn: 30-40% off DMC every 6-8 weeks. Best sale: Black Friday (40% off regular price, sometimes 50%). Also: Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day.
  • Michaels: 20-30% off with coupons (always check their app). 40% off one item coupons can be used for the DMC collector's tin.
  • Hobby Lobby: Rarely discounts DMC directly, but their 40% off one item applies to kits and accessories.
  • 123Stitch.com: No sales, but fair everyday prices and free shipping at $40.

Anchor Sales (UK/Europe)

  • Hobbycraft (UK): 3 for 2 on Anchor thread 2-3x/year. Best value.
  • LoveCrafts: Site-wide sales (15-25% off) during bank holidays.

Cosmo Sales

Rarely discounted. Best bet: Etsy bulk buys (5-10 packs) from Japanese sellers. Stitched Modern (US) occasionally runs 10-15% off site-wide.

Strategy: The Buy-As-You-Go Method

Don't buy all 456 colors upfront. When starting a project, use our floss matcher to check what you own vs what you need. Buy only the missing colors during the next sale. Over 2-3 projects, you'll build a complete collection without the upfront cost.
